# Privacy Policy **Last updated:** October 3, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how **Prayer Mode** collects, uses, and protects user data. Prayer Mode is designed to display prayer times and automatically manage the device's sound settings during prayer times according to the user's preferences. ## 1. Data We Collect Prayer Mode may access the following types of data to provide its core features: ### Location Data The app may access your approximate or precise location to calculate or display prayer times based on your current location. Location information may be used to: * Determine prayer times based on your location * Provide a daily prayer schedule appropriate for your location Location data is not sold or rented by the developer for advertising purposes. ### Device and App Information The app may process certain technical information to operate the application, analyze errors, and improve performance. This information may include device model, operating system version, app version, crash information, and technical information related to app performance. ### Advertising Data Prayer Mode may use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements within the application. Google and advertising service providers may process certain device-related or advertising-related information to display and measure advertisements. For more information about how Google processes data, please refer to Google's privacy policy. ## 2. How We Use Data Collected or accessed information may be used for the following purposes: * Providing prayer times * Determining prayer times based on location * Operating the core features of the application * Managing the device's sound settings according to the user's preferences * Monitoring and improving application performance * Detecting and fixing application errors * Displaying advertisements and measuring advertising performance ## 3. Device Sound Settings Prayer Mode may change the device's sound settings when the user has explicitly enabled the relevant features. This functionality is used to silence the device or manage its sound settings according to the user's preferences during prayer times. The application accesses device sound settings only to provide these features. ## 4. Data Sharing Prayer Mode does not sell or rent users' personal information. Third-party service providers used by the application may process certain technical or advertising-related information as necessary to provide their services. These services may include advertising services and services used to analyze application performance and errors. The data processing practices of third-party service providers are governed by their respective privacy policies. ## 5. Data Storage User preferences stored on the device may be used to ensure that the application functions properly. Settings and preferences created by the user may be stored locally on the device to provide the relevant application features. ## 6. Data Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to help protect user data against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. However, no method of data transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Children's Privacy Prayer Mode is not directed toward children of a specific age group. The application does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. ## 8. User Choices and Permissions Users can manage or disable the application's location, notification, and other permissions through their device's operating system settings. Disabling certain permissions may prevent some features of the application from functioning properly. ## 9. Third-Party Services Prayer Mode may use the following third-party services: * **Google AdMob** – Advertising and advertising measurement * **Firebase Crashlytics** – Application crash and error analysis * **Firebase Analytics** – Application usage and performance analytics The data processing practices of these third-party services are subject to their respective privacy policies. ## 10.

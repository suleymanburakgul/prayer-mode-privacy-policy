Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vaqtora: Adhan Volume Control collects, uses, and protects user data.

Vaqtora is designed to display prayer times and help users manage their device's sound settings during prayer times according to their preferences.

1. Data We Collect

Vaqtora may access the following types of data to provide its features.

Location Data

The app may access your approximate or precise location to calculate or display prayer times based on your location.

Location information may be used to:

  • Determine prayer times based on your location
  • Provide a daily prayer schedule appropriate for your location

The developer does not sell or rent location data for advertising purposes.

Device and App Information

The app may process technical information to operate the application, troubleshoot errors, and improve performance. This information may include device model, operating system version, app version, crash information, and technical performance data.

Advertising Data

Vaqtora may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device-related or advertising-related information to provide and measure advertisements.

2. How We Use Data

Data may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing prayer times
  • Determining prayer times based on location
  • Operating the app's features
  • Managing device sound settings according to user preferences
  • Monitoring and improving app performance
  • Detecting and fixing errors
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements

3. Device Sound Settings

Vaqtora may change device sound settings when the user has enabled the relevant features. This functionality helps silence the device or manage its sound settings during prayer times according to the user's preferences.

Access to sound settings is used to provide these app features.

4. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or rent users' personal information. Third-party providers may process certain technical or advertising-related information as necessary to provide their services.

Such providers handle information according to their own privacy policies and data processing practices.

5. Data Storage

User preferences and settings may be stored locally on the device to ensure the app's features work properly.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Vaqtora is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

8. User Choices and Permissions

Users can manage or disable location, notification, and other app permissions through their device settings. Disabling certain permissions may affect the availability or functionality of related features.

9. Third-Party Services

App crash and error analysis, if enabled in the app.

App usage and performance analytics, if enabled in the app.

Only services actually integrated into the app should be listed as being used. Each provider's own privacy policy governs its data processing practices.
